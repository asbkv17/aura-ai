About AURA AI

Adaptive User Recovery & Activity

AURA AI is an AI-powered wellness platform that makes health education, personal wellbeing and healthy lifestyle guidance easier to reach for everyone.

Mission

To make health knowledge and everyday wellbeing support accessible to everyone through responsible and adaptive artificial intelligence.

Who it's for

Teenagers, students and working adults, people who enjoy fitness, and older adults who want clear, friendly information about staying active and well.

Five main features

  • AI Assistant — plain-English answers to general health and lifestyle questions.
  • Health Library — categorized articles with source references.
  • My Progress — self-tracking of sleep, water, activity, habits and mood.
  • Fitness & Recovery — activity log, flexible goals and recovery tips.
  • Daily Wellness — mood check-in, private journal and habit list.

How the AI works

Every answer follows the same safe path, from your question to an answer you can check.

Step 1You ask a question in your own words.
Step 2The question is checked for urgent or unsafe topics.
Step 3Relevant approved Health Library articles are retrieved.
Step 4The AI writes a plain-English answer grounded in them.
Step 5You see the answer with links to its sources.

Privacy & Safety

Privacy, safety and responsible AI

Plain answers about what AURA AI does with your information and where its limits are.

AURA AI will

  • Give general, educational wellness information
  • Show sources when the library is used
  • Label AI-generated answers
  • Point you to professional help when needed

AURA AI will not

  • Diagnose a disease or condition
  • Prescribe medicine, treatment or doses
  • Tell you to ignore or delay care
  • Compare or rank users' bodies or activity

Your data in this demo

  • Where it's kept. Your profile, check-ins, activities, habits and journal are stored only in this browser on this device. They are not sent to a server.
  • What we collect. Only what you type in: a preferred name, optional interests and the entries you choose to add. We never ask for medical history, ID documents or your location.
  • AI questions. In live AI mode, your question, the last few messages of the chat and the matching library text are sent through our server to Google's Gemini API to write an answer. Chat messages are not stored by AURA AI. Please don't share personal medical details in the chat.
  • Deleting. Open your profile and choose “Delete my data” to remove everything at once. Each entry can also be edited or deleted on its own.
  • No advertising. Health-related entries are never used for advertising.

Younger users

AURA AI is designed to be safe for teenagers: answers use age-appropriate language, we collect the minimum information, and sensitive questions are directed to trusted adults and professionals. Younger users should use AURA AI with a parent or caregiver.

In the production version

The site already runs over HTTPS, keeps the AI key on the server and limits how many questions one visitor can send. The full release will add secure sign-in with password recovery, server-side storage where each user can only see their own entries, and input and file validation.

Terms of use

AURA AI is provided for general education and wellbeing support. Information may be incomplete and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified health professional. You are responsible for decisions about your health; when in doubt, ask a doctor or pharmacist.

If it's urgent: call your local emergency number (112 in many countries; 103 for an ambulance in Kazakhstan) or go to the nearest emergency department.
